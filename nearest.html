How far away is the nearest primate?

Enter an address or city, and we'll find the closest zoo, sanctuary, or research facility known to house non-human primates from our database of major US institutions.

This locator draws from a curated list of ~90 major AZA-accredited zoos and known primate sanctuaries in the US — it is not an exhaustive record of every location that houses a non-human primate (private owners and non-public research facilities aren't included). Distances are straight-line ("as the crow flies"), not driving distance. Address lookup uses the Photon geocoder (built on OpenStreetMap data); nothing you enter is stored by this site.
